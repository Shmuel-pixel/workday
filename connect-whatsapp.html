Connect WhatsApp Business

Before you start
  1. Do this on the iPad. Keep your iPhone next to you with WhatsApp Business open.
  2. When Meta asks, choose to connect your existing WhatsApp Business app number.
  3. Meta shows a QR code here. On the iPhone, in WhatsApp Business, scan it when it tells you to.
  4. Keep using WhatsApp Business on your phone like normal after this.
